Cosmopedia is a free space encyclopedia app. This policy explains what data the app and its third-party services handle. Cosmopedia has no user accounts, and the developer does not run a server that collects your personal data.

1. Data we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and we do not require sign-in. We do not sell personal data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cosmopedia shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use the following from your device: advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and OS information, app interaction data, and diagnostics. They use this to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve their services. Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

In regions where required (for example the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. You can change your choice from the app's settings where that option is shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads. See how Google uses data from partner apps.
